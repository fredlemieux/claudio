Recommendation

A (inline) is the most contextual — you see the diff right where the tool call happened.
B (badge) keeps the stream compact and only shows detail on demand.
C (drawer tab) is most like a real IDE "Source Control" panel — best if you want a full change summary after the run.

B or A+C combo is probably ideal: badge inline + aggregated tab for the full session diff.
